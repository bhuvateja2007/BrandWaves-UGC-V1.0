Executive Narrative

Brief Version 2.4

Lumino Audio is premiering the Apex-IV Wireless Headphone line. The objective is to highlight our industry-leading Adaptive Acoustic Shroud in organic transit settings: subway stations, cross-city commuter rails, bustling pedestrian crossings, and busy terminal platforms. Rather than polished studio ads, we are looking for documentary-grade, high-contrast creator perspectives that make viewers literally feel the noise melt away.

Tone & Feel Calm Amidst Chaos, Minimalist, Tactile
Key Product Focus Active Noise Cancellation + Matte Hardware
Audio Standard Natural Ambient Transition to Pristine Crisp VO

Production Matrix & Standards

Submissions deviating from these parameters will be routed back for revisions.

check

Mandatory Dos

  • done Natural Transit Audio Cut: Begin with authentic loud urban noise (train screech, crowd murmur), followed by a tactile tap on the right ear cup instantly dampening the background audio.
  • done Clear Product Close-Up: At least 4-6 seconds of focused b-roll highlighting the brushed aluminum hardware and Lumino debossed logo.
  • done High-Legibility Captions: Use clean, bold sans-serif burned-in captions for viewers watching without volume.
  • done Original Audio Track: Spoken creator voiceover describing physical comfort during transit.
close

Strict Don'ts

  • block No Visible Competitor Brands: Ensure no logos from Sony, Bose, or Apple are visible in the background or wear accessories.
  • block No Commercial Copyright Audio: Do not use unapproved viral pop music tracks; will result in ad account whitelisting flags.
  • block No Low-Light Grain: Ensure the video is recorded at 4K 60fps or high-bitrate 1080p. Avoid muddy underexposed subway lighting.
  • block No Overly Scripted Sales Speeches: Avoid reciting spec sheets verbatim. Focus on genuine creator sentiment.
